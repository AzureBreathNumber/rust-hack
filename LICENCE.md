## 🧩 About

**Rust Hack** is a comprehensive mod menu for Rust featuring ESP, aimbot, radar, loot ESP, no recoil, speed hack, and more.

Based on popular mods like **ESP**, **Aimbot**, and **Radar**.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Core Hacks
- ESP — see players, animals, and items through walls
- Aimbot — automatic targeting for precise shots
- Radar — real-time minimap for enemy movement
- Loot ESP — highlight valuable loot and crates

### 🎯 Combat Enhancers
- No Recoil — eliminate weapon recoil patterns
- No Spread — improve shot accuracy
- Speed Hack — increase movement speed (0.1x to 10x)
- Fly Hack — fly around the map freely

### 🛡️ Survival & QoL
- Item ESP — find resources and components easily
- Auto Loot — automatically collect nearby items
- Safe Zone Radar — avoid danger zones
- Time Control — adjust in-game time
